# Aria Android symbol assets

These SVGs are copied without modification from `hdi200/aria`, commit
`9dffd7f3feea6d5af068723d5cfff47e7283a69c`, under
`MuseReaderAndroid/Aria/Android/app/symbol-assets/aria/skip/Resources/Module.xcassets`.

The source `MuseReaderAndroid/scripts/generate-symbols.py` maps shared UI names
to Google Material Design icons (Apache License 2.0) and supplies custom paths.
They are the Android assets, not Apple's SF Symbols artwork.
